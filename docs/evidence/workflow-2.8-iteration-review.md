## Design and interpretation

Preserve prospective interpretation, failed results, immutable raw inputs,
reproduction, and decision history. Replace completion-by-counting with short
question/evidence/inference entries in existing cards. Separate writing from
internal audit records and apply boundaries to propositions rather than labels.
Reuse existing gate-closure states with reasons and evidence instead of erasing
historical failures. Stop revision when no remaining action could change a
material judgment.

The validator changes a bounded set of lexical/editorial findings to advisory
status, separates text-location checks from prescribed disclosure phrasing, and
reports its limited assurance. Numerical grounding, stale dependencies, source
resolution, withdrawal, and gate-closure protections remain executable.

## Judgment and limitations

The manual acceptance replay is in ../workflow-2.8-migration.md. It is an
implementer review of historical scenarios, not an independent manuscript cold
read. No new empirical analysis, historical PDF rebuild, submission checkpoint,
or prospective measurement of time saved was performed. Test success establishes
specified software behavior, not paper quality or the truth of historical claims.

Learned: finite text checks can remain useful prompts without controlling prose,
while important source and number errors remain detectable. Unresolved: whether
future users actually experience less rework and clearer papers. Stop kit changes
here unless a concrete regression or prospective reader/use evidence warrants
reopening. Decisions remain in the root decision-log.md.

## Cross-method sweep, 2026-10-05

Inspection covered all stage contracts; method facade/decision-tree/governance
routes; all nine pack prompts; targeted interpretation passages in pack details
and shared rules; shared writing, robustness and IS references; literature,
preregistration, review, talk and LaTeX companion entries; existing story,
literature and status templates. Findings were instruction conflicts, not new
estimates of research error frequency:

- Deliverables often stopped at code, diagnostics and a methods paragraph.
- Stage 2 defaulted to a theoretical move; story and status forms invited a
  mechanism even where accounting or measurement was the relevant logic.
- Stage 4 suggested moving a post-treatment variable into a mechanism test,
  without establishing a design for that inference.
- Shared robustness and the decision tree could forbid causal interpretation
  categorically while the FE/selection packs allowed a defended argument.
- Sensitivity and RD text could turn significance decisions into categorical
  design verdicts; the common diagnostic interpretation required more care.
- Talk instructions prescribed contribution counts. Editorial preferences could
  be read as binding even after the validator became advisory.

Repaired those paths and centralized general organization, economic meaning,
behavioral interpretation and contribution in the existing writing reference.
No method canon, estimation template, or external research project was changed;
this was not an audit of every statistical assertion in the methodological canon.
The transfer scenarios in the migration note describe prescribed decisions,
not independently observed behavior or measured workflow effectiveness.
