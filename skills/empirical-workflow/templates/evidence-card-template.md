# Evidence Card

Create one card per material claim, design choice, diagnostic, data source, or
result, reusing one module card with claim-specific locations when appropriate.
Do not duplicate a shared source in several forms. Distinguish observation from
inference and link the source artifact. Complete applicable fields, linking to
an existing specification or crosswalk rather than copying it.

## Verification and disposition

- Mechanical verification: method, artifact version, result, limits of coverage:
- Substantive review: exact claim, evidence bridge, reviewer, unresolved issues:
- Editorial review when relevant: reader understanding and proportionality:
- Issue status: active / repaired / closed by withdrawal / disclosed limitation:
- Closure reason, supporting evidence, affected current claims, and decision link:

Keep prior failures in the decision history. A completed field is not proof that
the underlying judgment is correct. Disclosed limitations cannot close an issue
while leaving an unsupported claim intact.
