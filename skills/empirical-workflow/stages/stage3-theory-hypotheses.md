# Stage 3: Theory and Hypotheses

## Inputs

- Router prerequisites and the Stage 1 feasibility record and Stage 2
  literature map, verified bibliography, and outlet-positioning memo.
- The proposed research question, available variation, and allowed designs.

## Automatic actions

- Read `references/research-writing.md`, applying the IS reference only when
  relevant, and complete
  `templates/paper-story-template.md` before interpreting estimates. Select one
  primary explanatory lens when needed; for measurement or accounting, state
  the organizing question without inventing a behavioral theory. State the
  actors, choices, constraints, competing explanations and economic consequence
  where applicable. Mark which links
  are observed, estimated, derived, or assumed.
- Define every focal construct, its level of analysis, proxy, and distinction
  from adjacent constructs. Check that theoretical scope does not exceed the
  data, design, or empirical unit.
- For causal claims, write the identifying-assumption sentence in language a skeptical reader can
  challenge, and map evidence and diagnostics that could support or fail it.
- Define confirmatory hypotheses before estimates: claim, expected sign, boundary condition,
  sample, specification, coefficient, and expected output.
- For confirmatory work, initialize
  `templates/preregistration-template.yaml` before the focal outcome is
  inspected. Bind each hypothesis to a claim revision, locked estimator,
  sample, clustering rule, acceptance-gate identifiers, and a falsification
  rule. Route registry-specific drafting through the companion `preregister`
  skill.
- Run a null-interest test: state why the question and contribution matter if
  the primary estimate is null. Name plausible competing explanations and
  a discriminating implication, or state why the data cannot distinguish them.
- Select the Stage 6 branch (reduced form, structural, or both) based on the
  question, not on anticipated results. Measurement, description, and institutional
  accounting use Stage 6a's evidence and target discipline without inventing a
  treatment effect; conditional model exercises use Stage 6b as applicable.
- Open each module with the question-evidence-inference contract in the router.
  In `docs/paper_story.md`, retain advisor requirements, withdrawn propositions,
  settled variable interpretations, and open questions with decision-log links.

## Required artifacts

- `docs/theory_hypotheses.md`: organizing question, explanatory or accounting
  logic, mechanism diagram or prose when applicable,
  identifying-assumption sentence, diagnostics plan, null-interest test,
  alternatives, and Stage 6 branch decision.
- `docs/hypothesis_estimate_map.md`: locked hypothesis-to-estimate map for
  confirmatory work, or question-to-evidence map for descriptive/accounting work;
  identify the target, sample, evidence, and expected exhibit as applicable.
- `docs/paper_story.md`: completed paper-story template with the single
  contribution headline, construct cards, bounded claim, exhibit sequence, and
  section-purpose matrix.
- `docs/checkpoints/checkpoint_a.md`: complete Checkpoint A record with
  evidence and a pass, revise, or pause outcome.
- A preregistration or pre-analysis record for confirmatory work, or an
  explicit exploratory designation. Retrospective registration is prohibited.
- Evidence cards for material theory and design claims, decision-log entries
  for commitments or deviations, and an updated `_status.md`.

## Red lines

- Lock the confirmatory hypothesis-to-estimate map before focal outcome
  inspection. Exploration is allowed when labeled and timed honestly; define its
  question and interpretation rules before the next analysis. Do not pretend it
  was prospective. Non-estimation work defines its target and measurement rules.
- Never substitute a method label for an identifying-assumption sentence, or
  omit alternatives merely because they are inconvenient.
- Pause for a recorded decision before changing the question, identification
  strategy, main specification, estimation sample, or Stage 6 branch.

## Exit condition

Checkpoint A passes. A revise or pause outcome does not authorize Stage 4. The
evidence strategy, knowledge value, alternatives, and applicable prospective
hypothesis-to-estimate or question-to-evidence map are complete, and the status record authorizes Stage
4 with outstanding risks visible.
